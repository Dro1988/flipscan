How to install

  1. Tap the install button above and download the APK.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser ("Install unknown apps").
  3. Open FlipScan and allow the camera — that's the barcode scanner.

What it does

  • Continuous barcode scan (or type a UPC / product name for damaged barcodes).
  • Shows the product, its typical resale price, and where the number came from.
  • Big-keypad shelf price → full math on screen: resale − eBay fees − shipping − your price = net profit, with a plain BUY / MAYBE / SKIP verdict.
  • Every scan lands on your haul list; tap items you bought and watch the session profit total.

Honest note on prices

Product ID and going asking prices come from UPCitemdb / Open Food Facts and work today. eBay sold prices are attempted three ways (price server, direct, in-app browser) and are always labeled by source on screen. When sold data can't be reached, the verdict says so and is capped at MAYBE rather than pretending. An official eBay developer key is the planned permanent fix for sold data.
